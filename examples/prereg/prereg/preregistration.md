# Preregistration (fictitious example for trust-ledger)

Registered 2026-03-01, before any data were collected.

## Hypothesis
H1: Participants who receive a worked example (condition B) score higher on the transfer test than
participants who do not (condition A).

## Design and sample
Between-subjects, two conditions, random assignment. N = 60 (30 per condition).

## Exclusions
Participants who fail the attention check are excluded before analysis. No other exclusions.

## Confirmatory analysis
Difference of mean scores, B minus A. One-sided permutation test with 10000 permutations
(random seed 1). H1 is supported if p < .05.

## Exploratory analyses
Anything else, including effects of age, is exploratory and will be labelled as such.
